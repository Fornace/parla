## Description

You can read a language and still freeze when someone speaks to you. Parla is
for that moment.

Pick a room: a café ten minutes before closing, a flat viewing, a delayed
train, your first morning at a new studio. Someone is there, in character, and
they start talking to you. You answer out loud. They react, ask something back,
and keep the conversation moving. They never correct you while you speak, never
grade you, never stop you to explain a rule.

When you end the conversation, Parla reads it back and tells you what to change:

- What landed. Two or three things you actually did well, quoted from your own
  words.
- A few small changes. The sentence you said, the sentence a native speaker
  would have said, and one line on why. Never more than you can hold.
- What to carry. Two or three phrases from the conversation, with a tap to hear
  them again.

Everything runs on your device with Apple Intelligence. Your voice is
transcribed locally, the conversation is generated locally, and nothing is ever
uploaded. There is no account and no subscription.

Fifteen languages: Spanish, French, Italian, German, Portuguese, Dutch,
Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Vietnamese and
English. Four levels, from first words to fluent.

A widget keeps the streak and the last phrase where you can see them, and Siri
or Shortcuts can open a room hands free.

Parla runs on any device with Apple Intelligence turned on.

## App Privacy

Data not collected. The app has no networking code at all: no analytics, no
crash reporting SDK, no accounts. Microphone audio and transcripts stay in the
app container.

## Review notes

Parla holds a spoken conversation with the on-device system language model
(Foundation Models). It requires Apple Intelligence to be enabled and the model
downloaded; the app shows a specific screen for each availability state, so a
reviewer without Apple Intelligence sees exactly what to turn on.

To test: open the app, tap Start talking, allow microphone and speech
recognition, and answer in the target language. Tap the close button to end, and
the debrief appears after a few seconds.

The microphone is used only while a room is open. Transcription is local
(SpeechAnalyzer with SpeechTranscriber); the conversation is generated by the
system model on device. No audio or text leaves the device, and the app contains
no network client.
